9. Receipt Scanning

The receipt scanner reads a photo of a receipt and suggests the store, date and total, and (with VIP Smart scan) the items and tax. It makes mistakes. Always check the figures; you are responsible for anything you save. Scanned receipts are not official records or tax documents and must not be relied on for any filing.

The basic scan runs on your device. Smart scan (VIP) sends the photo over a secure connection to our server and to an AI service provider only to read that receipt (see the Privacy Policy), and we ask for your OK before your first Smart scan.

Only scan receipts you have the right to scan. Do not scan IDs, bank statements, medical papers or other documents that are not receipts, and consider covering names, card numbers and phone numbers first. We apply a fair-use limit (currently 40 Smart scans a day per account) and may restrict misuse.
