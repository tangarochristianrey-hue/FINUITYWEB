FINUITY LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Version 3.0 · Last updated October 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

PCP-IS SOLUTIONS is the Personal Information Controller of FINUITY. Privacy contact / Data Protection Officer: pcpservicehelp@gmail.com. This policy follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

2. Personal Data We Handle

Account data: Email address, sign-in method (Google, email/password or guest), display name, and a unique account ID.

Data you enter: Income, expenses, wallet names and balances, loans (including names of people and notes), savings goals, budget limit, notes, and app settings. This also includes your Fin level and XP, streak, badges, weekly challenge progress and your reminder settings.

Payment and plan data: When you buy VIP we keep a payment record: your account ID, the plan, the amount, the date and status, a reference number from our payment provider, and your VIP expiry date. We do not receive or store your card number, e-wallet login or PIN. Those go to PayMongo, our payment provider.

Receipt data: If you use Smart receipt scan, the photo is sent for reading and is not stored by us. What is read (store, date, total, items, tax) is shown to you, and becomes your own entry only if you tap Use. We also keep a simple daily counter of Smart scans used by your account, to enforce the fair-use limit.

Security data: A salted hash of your PIN, your recovery question, and a salted hash of your answer (we cannot read either).

Technical data: Authentication tokens and data your browser stores (localStorage/sessionStorage: theme, tutorial status, unlock state, a cached copy of your VIP status, your free-scan counter, and notification permission). Google may receive your IP address and device details when you sign in, sync, or load fonts.

We do not collect: Bank or e-wallet credentials, card numbers, precise location, contacts or microphone data, and we do not run advertising or third-party analytics. We use your camera only while the scan screen is open, to take the receipt photo you choose; we do not record video or keep photos.

3. Why We Use It and Our Legal Basis

We process data to (1) create and secure your account, (2) store, sync and display your records across your devices, (3) provide backups, exports and reports you request, (4) process your VIP payment and switch your plan on, (5) read the receipts you ask us to scan, (6) send the reminder you turned on, and (7) keep the service safe, prevent fraud and abuse (such as fake payments or excessive scanning) and fix problems.

Our bases under Section 12 of the Data Privacy Act are your consent (given when you accept these documents and, for Smart scan, before your first scan), performance of the service or contract you asked for (including payments), compliance with legal obligations (such as tax and accounting records), and our legitimate interest in security and reliability.

We do not sell your data, use it for profiling or advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

4. Where Your Data Is Kept

A copy is stored in your browser on your device. If you use an account, a copy is also stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID. Our payment and receipt-reading functions run on Google Cloud servers (currently in Singapore). Google servers may be located outside the Philippines, so your data may be transferred and processed abroad; Google acts as our processor under its own security and data-protection commitments, and we remain accountable for your data.

5. Who We Share It With

Only with service providers needed to run the App:

Google: Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Google Sign-In, Google Fonts.

PayMongo: Our payment provider. The payment details you type at checkout go to PayMongo, which handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount and a reference, not your card or e-wallet credentials.

An AI service provider (currently Anthropic): For VIP Smart scan only. We send it the receipt photo and the list of expense categories, solely to read that receipt. We do not send your name, email, balances or other records.

jsDelivr: A public content network, which serves the open-source reader used by the basic on-device scan the first time you use it and may see your IP address.

We disclose data to authorities only when required by law or lawful order. We do not otherwise share, rent or sell personal data. The developer does not routinely view your individual records.

6. Receipt Photos and the Camera

The camera opens only when you tap Scan Receipt, and stops when you close it or take the photo.

Basic scan: The photo is read on your device and is not uploaded or saved.

Smart scan (VIP): After you say OK the first time, the photo is shrunk on your phone, sent over a secure connection to our server and then to the AI provider to be read, and is not saved by us.

Receipts can show other people's details (a cashier's name, a loyalty number, the last digits of a card); cover anything you do not want read. You can always use the basic scan instead, and you always review the result before anything is added.

7. How Long We Keep It

Local data stays until you clear it or your browser storage. Cloud data stays until you delete it or your account, which you can do any time in Settings (Legal and Data). Backups you download are yours to manage.

Receipt photos sent for Smart scan are not kept. Payment records are kept as long as tax, accounting or other laws require, even if you delete your account. Your VIP and scan-counter records are removed when we process your account deletion. Inactive guest accounts may be removed after a long period. Deleted data may persist briefly in provider backups before being overwritten.

8. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS in transit, Google's access controls, and salted hashing (SHA-256) for your PIN and recovery answer. Payments are confirmed by a signed message from the payment provider, and VIP status can only be written by our server, not by the App.

Your records are not end-to-end encrypted, and the PIN is a convenience lock: someone with access to your unlocked device may see your data.

Optional biometric unlock: If you turn it on, your device's operating system checks your face or fingerprint through the browser's WebAuthn feature; the App never receives, stores or transmits your face or fingerprint data, and keeps only an on-device credential reference in this browser. Your PIN always still works.

No system is perfectly secure; use a device passcode and keep backups.

9. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act you may: be informed; object to processing; access your data; correct it; request erasure or blocking; withdraw consent; receive your data in a portable format; and claim damages for violations.

In-app: Edit any entry, download a JSON/CSV backup, or permanently delete your account and cloud data in Settings.

Otherwise email PCPSERVICE@gmail.com; we aim to respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Withdrawing consent or deleting your account means we can no longer provide cloud sync, and we may keep the payment records we are legally required to keep.

10. Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach that puts you at risk occurs, we will notify the NPC and affected users within 72 hours of knowledge or reasonable belief, as required by NPC rules, and tell you what happened and what to do.

11. Other People's Data

If you enter details about others (such as a person who owes you money, or a name that appears on a receipt you scan), do so only as needed for your personal records and keep them accurate. You are responsible for that entry.

12. Children

The App is not directed to children. Users under 18 (including students) need a parent or guardian to accept these documents, supervise their use and approve any purchase. If we learn we hold a minor's data without proper consent, we will delete it on request.

13. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser storage and Firebase tokens that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and remember settings, including your theme, a cached copy of your VIP status, your free-scan counter and your reminder time. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

14. Changes and Contact

We will update this policy when practices or the law change, show the new version, and ask for acceptance again where required.
